# DK Kinehall

**DK Kinehall (ДК Кинехолл)** is a free, standalone Linux media player by **DK Softworks**.

Watch movies, television episodes, and listen to your own music in one application.

## Features

- **Local playback:** Open movies and music stored on your computer, including MKV, MP4, AVI, WebM, MP3, and FLAC files.
- **Up Next:** Queue videos, skip between them, and optionally remove completed videos automatically.
- **Embedded subtitles:** Choose subtitle tracks contained inside an MKV from the **Subtitles** menu or by right-clicking the picture. Subtitles are off by default.
- **Audio tracks:** Switch between available audio tracks in the right-click menu.
- **External subtitles:** Add a subtitle file separately when needed.
- **Playback controls:** Play, pause, stop, previous, next, seek, and jump backward or forward 10 seconds.
- **Fullscreen:** Double-click the picture or use the fullscreen control.
- **Volume and speed:** Adjust playback speed and volume up to 100%.
- **Resume playback:** Continue a previously opened movie from its saved position.
- **Artwork:** Display matching local poster or cover artwork when available.
- **Languages:** English and Russian interface options.

## Getting started

Open DK Kinehall and choose **Open files** to play a video or song.

Use **+ Add files** to place additional media in **Up Next** without interrupting the current movie. Use **Choose folder** to add media from a folder.

For subtitles already included in an MKV, open **Subtitles** and select the desired track. You do **not** need to choose a separate subtitle file.

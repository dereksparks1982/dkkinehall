# DK Kinehall master development and acceptance roadmap

Updated 2026-11-27. This is the authoritative running backlog. Keep every unfinished feature here until accepted. **Source added** means committed, not tested or installed. **Planned** means not yet implemented. Maintain this file and README at every build.


## Owner directive: world-standard player and optional media server (2026-11-27)

**Priority:** match all practical VLC capabilities, then exceed them through integrated cinema/library/TV features, add-on interoperability and an optional personal media server. This is an engineering objective, not a claim of market leadership or completed parity. Every item below is a mandatory tracked requirement or audit area, even where existing source implements part of it. Do not remove earlier backlog. Standalone local playback remains usable without a server, internet connection, subscription, account or additional hardware; optional server mode may run on the same computer or a dedicated user-chosen machine.

### Permanent product commitments
- Free core, extensions infrastructure, server features and clients; no first-party advertising, subscriptions, premium unlocks, mandatory central accounts, sale of viewing data or compulsory cloud telemetry. User-controlled diagnostics and local accounts for optional server security. External media sources may carry their own ads, access conditions or costs; never promise control over those.
- User-owned media, local metadata, open import/export, portable backups, reversible migration, no vendor lock-in. Preserve Movies gold, TV Shows white, Live TV brown and all approved visual/language conventions. Audio-only playback remains supported without reintroducing a Music library category without owner approval.
- Support open standards first, license review for every dependency and add-on, no unauthorized access or DRM bypass. GPL code cannot be incorporated under conflicting no-commercial-redistribution terms; use compatible licensing or independent implementations and preserve notices. No changes to existing project license without owner approval.
- Maintain one main branch and owner-authorized builds, README and roadmap updates, normal installed update workflow. Source, runtime verified, and owner accepted are distinct statuses. No fake test builds; no blanket claims that every codec, source or extension works.

### VLC parity audit: required before a 1.0 parity claim
Reference baseline: VideoLAN's official feature matrix (https://www.videolan.org/vlc/features.html), desktop documentation (https://docs.videolan.me/vlc-user/desktop/3.0/en/) and advanced guides (https://docs.videolan.me/vlc-user/desktop/3.0/en/advanced/index.html). Maintain a version-pinned granular capability matrix with supported/partial/missing/unsupported-by-design, rationale, platform, implementation location and reproducible real-media acceptance evidence. Audit released VLC capabilities and update as VLC evolves; no claim of exhaustive verified coverage until checked.

1. **Input and codecs:** local files, directories, drag/drop, archives and supported image sequences; MPEG PS/TS, MP4/MOV, Matroska/WebM, AVI, ASF/WMV, FLV, Ogg, RealMedia, MXF, raw elementary streams, legacy formats, corrupt/truncated media recovery; AVC/H.264, HEVC, AV1, VP8/9, MPEG-1/2/4, VC-1, Theora, DV, ProRes, DNx, MJPEG, Cinepak and other bundled decoder coverage; AAC, MP3, FLAC, ALAC, Vorbis, Opus, AC-3/E-AC-3, DTS, PCM, WMA, TrueHD and other audio formats. Inspect actual packaged demuxers, protocols, decoders, encoders and hardware backends; capability detection rather than marketing promises.
2. **Physical media and devices:** DVD/ISO/VIDEO_TS menus, titles, angles, chapters, audio/subtitle selection; Blu-ray/BDMV, unprotected disc/image playback, audio CD/CD-Text, VCD/SVCD; permitted capture from cameras, microphones, capture cards, screen/window and supported DVB/ATSC tuners (ATSC 1.0 and feasible later standards; DVB-T/T2/S/S2/C), HDHomeRun; channel scan, tuning, programme selection and radio playback. No extra hardware for internet TV.
3. **Network input and discovery:** HTTP(S), HLS, DASH, RTSP/RTP/RTCP, UDP unicast/multicast, SAP/SDP, SRT, RIST, RTMP, MMS where feasible, FTP/SFTP, SMB, NFS, UPnP/DLNA, Bonjour/mDNS, IPv6; M3U/M3U8, PLS, XSPF, ASX, CUE and other playlist formats; authenticated sources, proxy options, TLS validation, controlled local network grants and per-protocol cache settings.
4. **Playback and navigation:** accurate seeking, frame-step in supported directions, A/B loops, repeat/shuffle, bookmarks, jump to time, DVD title/program/track selection, gapless or continuous playback where feasible, speed/pitch, independent external audio, multi-instance optional, multiple video tracks, screenshots and configurable hotkeys, mouse gestures, touch and accessibility. Preserve existing single-window default.
5. **Video rendering:** aspect ratio/crop/zoom/pan/rotation/flip, fit/stretch, always-on-top, picture-in-picture, fullscreen and multi-monitor output, optional wallpaper video where supported; deinterlacing/postprocessing, scaling/chroma, brightness/contrast/gamma/saturation/hue, sharpen/denoise, color adjustment, overlays, logo/text, mirror, mosaic, 360-degree and stereoscopic viewing; HDR10/HLG tone mapping, wide gamut and color management, refresh-rate/frame pacing, hardware acceleration and direct rendering (VA-API, platform-specific backends as platforms are supported), codec passthrough and fallback.
6. **Audio:** 0–200% volume, device selection, stereo/mono/channel mapping, balance, equalizer presets and custom bands, compressor/limiter/normalization, dynamic range control, pitch-preserving time stretch, replay gain, spatial/binaural and 5.1/7.1 output, compatible digital audio passthrough, delay synchronization, external audio, visualizations, microphone input and audio-only listening.
7. **Subtitles and accessibility:** embedded and external subtitle tracks, SRT/ASS/SSA/WebVTT/TTML and legacy text, VobSub/PGS/DVB bitmap and broadcast closed captions/teletext where supported, font/size/color/position/style, character encoding, delay, rate and synchronization, search/download with permission, generated subtitles and translation/voiceover; screen reader/keyboard and high-contrast accessibility. Preserve English and complete Russian localization.
8. **Conversion and capture tools:** remux, transcode and export with selectable container/codec/quality/resolution/bitrate/audio/subtitle settings, profiles, batch queue, progress/cancel, audio extraction, trimming/joining, image export, camera/mic/screen recording, permitted disc conversion, simultaneous streaming/transcoding, broadcast recording. No source overwrite by default.
9. **Stream output and administration:** send local/authorized media over HTTP, UDP, RTP/RTSP and supported transport formats; multicast/discovery, playlist broadcasting, concurrent channels, screen sharing, mosaic, overlays, VLM-like scheduling/control, local network casting and renderer discovery, optional headless CLI, remote control API with authentication, scripting/automation hooks.
10. **Operational maturity:** codec/media information, logs with secrets removed, dropped frames/bitrate/network statistics, advanced preferences, reset/export/import profiles, plugin manager, updates, platform packaging, file associations, resource efficiency and crash recovery. Linux first; evaluate Windows/macOS and mobile/TV clients separately without interrupting the standalone work. Compare startup/seek latency, decode correctness, CPU/GPU load, memory, long-run stability, subtitle sync, recovery, power use and stream handling against a pinned VLC release on identical files and hardware.

## Local Library Home scope

- **New standalone requirement:** automatically discover movie/TV files under the user's home and standard mounted-media roots, without a folder-picker setup. A bounded one-shot scan follows immediate rendering of the saved IndexedDB collection; manual Scan Library remains available. Never run a constant scanner/server.
- **Filesystem actions:** right-click an individual movie or episode for Delete from Disk. The Electron main process validates the file path and requests OS confirmation before offering Trash or permanent removal. Permanent deletion requires additional confirmation. No bulk series deletion; never just hide the item.
- **Native-to-original-player path:** Electron returns a validated file URL and current file size and timestamp to the existing original video element; native MKV metadata is read through capped byte ranges. Native format conversion is restricted to the actual bundled decoder, not fake Blob remuxing. The original window appearance, background, controls and icons stay unchanged.
- **Acceptance still outstanding:** validate native file URL access and the range-reader in the actual Electron runtime on Linux, mount-disconnect recovery, scanning performance, and deletion/trash behavior before releasing the updater. Routine installed-player updates now use the single standard updater; full replacement codec packages remain separately gated. Do not provide terminal commands or downloads until requested.

- Start on **Library Home** in one native window. The existing Chromium player is a persistent internal view, not a second window or replacement; switching views preserves playback. Preserve the exact player background, approved icons and styles.
- **Movies / TV Shows only**, including Season 0 specials when present and excluding audio-only Ogg files. Nested Show/Season/01 - Episode folder layouts are classified as TV, and a metadata-only alias supplies proper series/episode details to the original player without renaming video files. Continue Watching above the entire selected collection; TV series group by title with season/episode drilldown. No music library, no Recently Added and **no media server**.
- Cache folder handles and catalog records in IndexedDB, plus separate series posters and episode stills and verified episode titles that remain cached independently of artwork. Generic poster.jpg is not assigned from a mixed collection root; dedicated series/movie folders are preferred. Scans use folder-specific database indexes; poster requests are deduplicated across Continue Watching and the TV series collection. Video files never enter the cache. Remember playing position and completed status between application launches.
- Verified episode-name changes update only matching card text without re-rendering the entire Home grid.
- Resume state handles short videos without accidentally marking Stop at 0:00 as watched; verified episode titles remain available when local poster artwork is already cached.
- Entire Movies/TV poster collection loads progressively as the user scrolls. Continue Watching refreshes are deferred while Home is unfocused, reducing work during movie playback on the Surface Pro.
